# RSS reader

Status: implemented

## Scope

A standard RSS/Atom reader, independent of novel sources. OPML exchanges subscription lists and groups; it is not an article or parsing-rule format. Legado `rssSource` webpage adapters are outside this module.

## Interfaces

The core owns feeds and articles (`crates/core/src/rss.rs` for fetching and content, `rss/filter.rs` for 过滤规则, `rss/search.rs` for 搜索订阅, `storage/rss.rs` and `storage/rss_filters.rs` for the tables `rss_feeds`, `rss_articles`, `rss_groups`, `rss_cleared`, `rss_filters`, and the search index `rss_search`, `rss_commands.rs` for the commands). The frontend reaches them through `FeedService` (`src/services/contracts.ts`) and keeps 订阅's state in `FeedStore` (`src/application/rss.ts`). Articles and feed identities are separate from novel books and `ReaderLocator`.

Commands: `list_feeds` (with unread and total counts, whether each is enabled, and its own settings), `search_feeds` (words or an address), `preview_feed` (a feed's newest articles, stored nowhere), `add_feed`, `update_feed` (title, group, a new address, enabled, own settings), `remove_feeds`, `list_feed_groups`/`save_feed_groups`/`rename_feed_group`/`remove_feed_group`, `refresh_feeds` (the enabled feeds named, or all; with `due`, those whose interval has passed; progress as `rss-refresh` events, cancellable), `list_articles` (one feed, one group, or all; 全部, 未读, or 星标; a search over titles, authors, and text; pages by publication time), `get_article`, `fetch_full_text`, `set_article_state` (read, starred, 保持未读), `mark_all_read`, `mark_read_range`, `list_feed_filters`/`save_feed_filter` (with `purge`)/`remove_feed_filter`/`test_feed_filter`, `rss_image`, `import_opml`, `export_opml_file`, and `get_rss_settings`/`save_rss_settings` (`rss.json` on the device). Its tables came with schema 29; each feed's own settings, ordered groups, 保持未读, and cleared articles with schema 30; 过滤规则, 未读不清理 for each feed, and the search index with schema 31.

## Behavior

Feeds are fetched through the app's network settings (proxy and DoH), with `If-None-Match`/`If-Modified-Since`, following up to five redirects. `feed-rs` parses RSS 0.9–2.0, RSS 1.0, Atom, and JSON Feed. Requests carry a browser's `User-Agent` and `Accept-Language`, and a connection the other end drops is tried once more. An article's id is a hash of the feed's first address (`id_base`, which a feed given a new address keeps) and the entry's id (its link, title, or text when it has none), so repeated entries count once and a refresh updates articles it has while keeping their read and starred state. A failed feed records its error and keeps its articles; refreshing runs four feeds at once. Each feed keeps its newest `keepArticles` articles (200, 500 by default, 1000, 2000, or all) or those of the last `keepDays` days (30, 90 by default, 180, or 365), whichever is more; entries too old to keep are not counted as new. Starred articles stay until unstarred, also after their feed is removed, and articles kept unread are never pruned; with 未读文章不自动清理 (or the feed's own setting) unread articles are not pruned either.

过滤规则 are regular expressions (the `regex` crate, its compiled size limited, so a rule cannot be slow or huge; a rule that does not compile is refused when saved). Each matches the title, the author, the text without markup, or all three, for every feed, a group, or one feed; it drops the articles it matches, or (只保留) lets in only those. An article is stored when no drop rule matches it and, if keep rules apply, one of them does. Rules apply only to articles not stored yet, when a fetch is stored, so the server filters what it fetches the same way and a device taking the server's articles checks them again; articles kept out are not remembered, so a rule removed lets them in on the next refresh. A rule is tried on the newest 2000 stored articles it applies to before saving (how many it would keep out, and some of their titles), and may be saved removing the stored ones it would have kept out, starred ones excepted. A feed's new address and a group's new name take their rules along; removing a feed or a group removes its rules. Rules sync as `rss-filter:<id>` records and go into backups.

Search uses an FTS5 index with the trigram tokenizer (`rss_search`, contentless, keyed by the articles' rowids, which nothing renumbers), so words in any language are found inside text: titles, authors, and the text without markup (the 全文 once read, else the feed's), its first 20,000 characters. The core writes an article's entry when it is stored, changed, given 全文, or restored as a star; a trigger removes it with the article. Searches of one or two characters, too short for trigrams, look at titles and excerpts. A test of 50,000 articles in 50 feeds keeps the first page, ten pages, a group's unread page, a search, the unread counts, and 全部标为已读 under 300 ms each in a debug build. Article lists make only the rows near the view (measured as they are laid out, estimated before), and unread counts over 999 show as 999+. The picture cache keeps under its limit (100 MB, 500 MB by default, 1 GB, or none): after each 10 MB written it is measured, and over the limit the pictures looked at longest ago go until it is at 90%.

Each feed has its own settings, each empty one following 订阅设置: its refresh interval, how many articles and days it keeps, whether opening an article reads its 全文, and whether 全文 is always read by the server. A stopped feed (启用更新 off) is never refreshed, even by name, and keeps its articles. A new address is read once first and must be a feed; the feed's articles, read and starred marks move with it, and other devices move theirs when the change syncs. Groups are names on feeds, ordered by `rss_groups` (where empty groups also live); renaming into an existing group merges the two, and removing one leaves its feeds ungrouped or unsubscribes them.

添加订阅 is a search. Words look in Feedly's public directory (`cloud.feedly.com/v3/search/feeds`, no account), shown with the site, followers, and description. An address is read as a feed; if it is not one, the feeds its page names (`<link rel="alternate">`) or its site's usual feed paths answer, and then the RSSHub instance's Radar rules (`/api/radar/rules`, kept in `rsshub-rules.json` for a day, the old copy serving while the instance cannot be reached) turn it into routes: rules are found by the address's site (any suffix of its host) and subdomain (`www` and the bare domain stand for each other), their path patterns (`:name`, `:name?`, `*`) are matched against the page's path, and the values fill the route; a route needing a value the page does not give is left out. With an access key set, it is written into the addresses made, so the server and every device can refresh them. Any result opens a preview of the feed's newest articles (up to 30, sanitized, stored nowhere), which can be read, then 订阅 with a name and a group. OPML import subscribes to each outline with an `xmlUrl`, filed under the path of the outlines around it joined by ` / `; addresses already subscribed are left alone. Export writes OPML 2.0 with groups as outlines.

Article HTML is sanitized once, when stored: an allowlist of reading elements and attributes, links and images made absolute (http, https, and mailto only), lazy-loading `data-src` taken as the image, and scripts, styles, frames, forms, and media dropped with their content. 全文 reads the article's page in its declared charset, scores paragraph containers by text, punctuation, class names, and link density, and keeps the best one sanitized; when nothing long enough stands out the excerpt and link stay and the reader is told why. When the page cannot be reached (a network error) while connected, or always for a feed set to, the [server](server.md) reads it (`POST /api/rss/fulltext`), never at a local or private address. Images load through `rss_image`, with the article as referrer, into `rss-images/` (订阅文章图片 in 数据管理), so they show offline. 数据管理 also shows 订阅文章 (the articles' text): clearing it drops read articles that are not starred and the 全文 kept of the rest, and remembers the dropped ones (for a year) so a refresh does not bring them back unread.

订阅 refreshes when entered if the last refresh is over ten minutes old (unless 进入订阅时刷新 is off), on pulling down (phone) or 刷新, and while the app is visible, each minute, the feeds whose interval has passed (off, 30 minutes, 1 hour by default, 3 or 6 hours, or 5–1440 minutes of one's own; a feed's own interval counts with the setting off). There is no background refresh. Read and starred changes show at once and are saved behind; a failed save puts them back, as it does for settings. Opening an article marks it read unless turned off, and fetches its 全文 when 打开文章时读取全文 (or the feed's own setting) is on (quietly keeping the feed's text if that fails). With 划出屏幕时标为已读, unread articles scrolled up out of the list are marked read together a second later; they stay in place until the list loads again. An article's long-press menu marks it read or unread, stars it, marks the list's articles above or below it read (it included), and sets 保持未读: nothing marks such an article read (opening, scrolling, ranges, 全部标为已读) but the reader marking it. Read articles show dimmed, unread ones with a dot and a bold title. Article lists show each article's first picture, loaded once in view, unless turned off. Article text uses its own size setting.

订阅设置 is reached from 设置 › 订阅 (beside 管理订阅) and from 订阅's menu. It holds the interval, 进入订阅时刷新, 打开文章时标为已读, 划出屏幕时标为已读, 打开文章时读取全文, 文章列表显示图片, the text size, 最多保留, 文章保留, 未读文章不自动清理, 图片缓存上限, 过滤规则 (every rule; 订阅详情 lists those that apply to its feed and adds rules for it), and the RSSHub instance (`https://rsshub.app` by default) with its access key; whether 订阅 shows in the tab bar is in 外观设置. These settings stay on the device. 管理订阅 adds feeds, opens 分组管理, and lists each feed (refresh it, or open its 订阅详情, also reached by long-pressing it in 订阅). The server's own refresh interval is set in 定时追更. The core's errors and log entries carry keys (`error.rss*`, `log.rss*`, `log.feedsRefreshed`), so they show in the interface language.

Subscriptions (with their own settings and whether they are enabled), the group order (`rss-group:<name>`), read articles, and starred articles sync ([Sync](sync.md), [decision](../notes/implemented/2026-10-14-rss-article-state.md)) and go into backups (subscriptions and starred articles) under the RSS 订阅 category. While connected, a refresh asks the [server](server.md) to refresh and takes the articles it fetched; feeds it cannot give are fetched on the device. The server refreshes, each minute, its feeds whose interval has passed: their own, or its 定时追更 interval. Layouts belong to [interaction design](../design/interaction.md).

## Acceptance

- RSS 2.0, Atom, and RDF parse with repeated and malformed entries; HTML is sanitized; discovery finds linked and guessed feeds.
- Refresh keeps state and articles through 304 answers and failures; retention keeps starred articles.
- OPML with nested groups imports, exports, and reimports without duplicates.
- Unread and starred filtering, paging, search, optimistic state, and the refresh schedule are tested in the store; the phone flow opens a feed, reads, stars, and fetches 全文.
- Two devices and a server converge on subscriptions and read state with concurrent marks; a connected device refreshes through the server.
- A feed given a new address keeps its articles and marks on every device; stopped feeds are not refreshed; each feed's interval decides when it is due; groups keep their order through renames, merges, and removal.
- 以上/以下标为已读, 全部标为已读, and opening leave articles kept unread; clearing 订阅文章 keeps unread and starred articles and does not bring read ones back.
- 全文 falls back to the server, which refuses local and private addresses.
- Filters drop and keep by field and scope, refuse bad and oversized expressions, test and purge stored articles, follow renamed feeds and groups, and sync and back up.
- Search finds words inside titles, authors, text, and 全文 in Chinese and English, falls back for short searches, forgets removed articles, and indexes existing articles on upgrade; 50,000 articles stay quick.
- Pruning keeps unread articles when asked; the picture cache drops the least recently seen beyond its limit.
- Radar rules match optional parameters and wildcards, skip routes missing values, and carry the key; Feedly results parse; a preview stores nothing.
- Back closes an article before its list; the long-press menu, 订阅详情, 分组管理, the custom interval, marking read on scrolling in the windowed list, 搜索订阅 with a preview and subscribing, 过滤规则, and 999+ are tested in the interface.

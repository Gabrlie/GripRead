# Interface languages

Status: implemented

## Scope

The interface is shown in 简体中文 (the default) or English, chosen on the device. Book content, source rules and their data (such as Legado's check-failure groups), and log lines are not interface text and stay as they are.

## Interfaces

**Dictionaries:** `src/i18n/` owns them, one folder per language with a file per area (common, settings, shelf, reader, sources, cloud, and so on).
- `zh-CN` defines the keys.
- Each English area `satisfies` its Chinese area's keys, so a missing or extra string fails the typecheck.
- A test checks that each key lives in one area, and that English keeps the same placeholders.

**Reading texts:**
- Pages read text through `t(key, params)`.
- `{name}` placeholders take parameters, and `{count|one|other}` picks an English plural.
- Dates and times come from `formatDateTime`, `formatDate`, and `formatClock` in the chosen locale.
- Constant label tables are functions or getters, so they are read when rendered.

**Errors:**
- Errors from the core (`AppError`) and from the server's JSON responses carry an optional `key` with `params` next to their Chinese `message`.
- `errorText` translates `error.<key>` and falls back to the message. Command errors are translated as they arrive, so every way of showing them follows the language.
- The core needs no language setting.

## Behavior

**Choosing the language:**
- 界面语言 under 设置 › 外观 offers 跟随系统, 简体中文, and English.
- The choice is kept in browser storage on the device, never synced or backed up.
- The app root renders again when the language changes, so open screens follow at once.
- The web version follows the browser unless chosen there.
- The page's `lang` attribute follows the choice.

**What follows the language:**
- Counts of characters use 万字 in Chinese and thousands in English.
- Title sorting keeps Chinese collation.
- The core's source-check stages are translated by the interface.
- Messages from source engines and log lines stay Chinese.

## Acceptance

- Switching language changes open screens at once and survives a restart.
- Every key exists in every language with the same placeholders, and in one area only (typecheck and tests).
- An error with a key shows in the chosen language; one without shows its message.

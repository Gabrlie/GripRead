# Interaction and visual design

Status: planned

## Visual language

The existing page implementation is the visual baseline. The reader geometry and controls described in the [active import and reading plan](../plans/2026-09-23-import-reader-experience.md) are an intentional update to that baseline; preserve its paper surfaces, tone-specific accents, editorial headings, fine separators, rounded cards, desktop sidebar, and floating phone navigation elsewhere. Use the colors in the [theme stylesheet](../../src/styles.css) and [Tailwind configuration](../../tailwind.config.js) as the single token source. Bundled reading fonts and local SVG icons keep reading independent of external asset requests.

Service integration does not authorize redesigning or removing page regions outside the reader update in the active plan. Library retains its unified glass header, group strip, four-line list cards, and cover grid with three phone columns and five desktop columns. Discovery retains search, source pills, eight category tiles, and the recommendation region. Subscriptions retain channel and article regions. Settings keeps an overview card, grouped rows, a segmented theme control, and paper swatches that follow the light or dark theme; 关于 opens a page with the version, an introduction, and a reserved update and change-log area. A long press selects text only in reader text, introductions, and fields; elsewhere it is a press. Confirmations and errors appear in one floating toast above pages and dialogs, never inside a page's layout, and disappear after a few seconds (errors and 撤销 offers a little longer); an error notice offers 查看日志, which opens the log over the current page. Books without a cover get a plain paper-toned cover with a thin frame and the title. The reader fills the available application viewport with compact insets and safe-area handling, readable desktop line length, unobtrusive metadata, overlay toolbars, and a compact typography panel; it does not use a vertically inset page card.

Unavailable capabilities remain visible and disabled. Preserve the current page hierarchy, card components, and control geometry; replace fixture identities and statistics with truthful unavailable content inside their existing regions. The shelf group strip uses only the service snapshot, including an empty group list; discovery categories are a separate presentation taxonomy. Remove fabricated books, feeds, statistics, and success responses without replacing whole screens with a single unavailable message. Empty content regions use truthful empty states within the existing layout. Completed capabilities keep their established controls and consume real service state.

Novel typography starts at 18 px with 1.8 line height. Measure each page inside the actual available viewport with compact reading insets and safe areas, and keep desktop line length readable. Do not permanently reserve toolbar-sized top or bottom padding. Toolbars may temporarily overlay the page; showing or hiding them, or switching panels, must not repaginate the text or change its saved position. Motion parameters belong exclusively to [motion design](motion.md). Future screen capabilities below remain planned and do not supersede the current visual baseline without an explicit design request.

The glass header and desktop sidebar use background alpha 0.45 in light mode, 0.58 in dark mode, and 0.65 in OLED mode; the floating dock uses 0.52, 0.64, and 0.70 respectively. Keep blur at 20 px and saturation at 180%, preserve foreground opacity and existing borders/shadows, and share the header material across the shelf title and group strip. These values reveal in-app content without changing native window transparency. Dropdown material remains more opaque for readability.

The application icon is an open hardcover book with a visible page block and a blue cover on a white ground. Its sources are in [`src-tauri/icons/source`](../../src-tauri/icons/source/icons.json): the desktop tile, and the Android adaptive foreground and background. `pnpm tauri icon src-tauri/icons/source/icons.json` regenerates the desktop and Android icons; the pre-Android 8 launcher icons and the web icons in `public/` are rendered from the same sources, since the generator crops them too tightly.

## Navigation and screens

Phone navigation has Library, Find Books, RSS, and Settings tabs, displayed in the product's default locale. Desktop uses the same destinations in a sidebar. Novel reading hides global navigation; returning restores the previous selection and list position. On the desktop, RSS shows subscriptions, articles, and the article in three columns that scroll on their own.

| Screen | Phone | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Library | Continue-reading card, cover grid/list, long-press management | Recent reading, grid/list, multi-select, file drop |
| Find Books | Search, source scope, progressive grouped results, detail navigation | Results and selected book details side by side |
| RSS | Groups, subscriptions, articles, and article view through successive screens | Subscription, article list, and content columns; collapsible side panels |
| Settings | Reading overview (opens 阅读记录), appearance (theme, paper tone, OLED black), rules (书源管理, 替换净化, TXT目录规则), backup and cloud (备份与恢复, 云书架), 日志 (level filter, 详细日志, copy, share, clear), about | Category navigation beside settings |

Use shadcn/ui controls and accessible primitives. Phone actions normally use a Drawer; desktop uses a Dialog or side panel. Closing a panel restores focus and reading context rather than reopening the book. The Android back action closes the topmost dialog or reader panel, then hides visible reader toolbars, then leaves the reader, then returns to Library, and finally exits after pending saves finish.

## Library and discovery

Show a single continue-reading entry before the shelf. Support custom groups and unread/reading/finished filters. Book items expose title, progress, updates, and local availability. Cloud Library appears when configured and represents the selected WebDAV folder; [Library](../subsystems/library.md) owns availability actions and [Sync](../subsystems/sync.md) owns transfer semantics.

Find Books retains successful results when one source fails. Book details show source, title, author, cover, introduction, latest chapter, and a contents entry. Offer add-to-shelf, read, update check, chapter cache, and source switching according to available capabilities. Unsupported operations explain their cause.

Empty Library offers local file import; an empty 订阅 offers 添加订阅 and OPML import. Do not require cloud setup before these actions.

书源管理 follows Legado 3.26.7's source list with GripRead styling: a search field (name, address, group, comment), filter chips (全部, 已启用, 已禁用, 需登录, 未分组, 已启用发现, 已禁用发现, then each group), a sort menu with 反序, and rows showing the name, explore and login marks, groups, address, and the response time or the last check error. Each row has an enable switch and a menu (编辑, 置顶, 置底, 登录, 调试, 启用/禁用发现, 校验, 删除). The header adds 新建, sort, and More (网络导入 with remembered addresses, 本地导入, 粘贴导入, 多选, 校验显示的书源, 导出全部). Imports list each source as 新增, 更新, or 已有 with checkboxes and options to keep local names, groups, and switches. Multi-select shows a bottom bar of batch actions. A check runs in the background with a progress card and a stop button; its dialog's 禁用失效的书源 (on by default) switches off a source whose search, details, contents, or text fail, and only its explore when just 发现 fails. The editor has tabs 基本, 搜索, 发现, 详情, 目录, 正文, and JSON, a save button, and More (保存并调试, 登录, 复制源, 粘贴源, 设置源变量, 清除 Cookie), and asks before discarding changes. 调试 streams each step of search → details → contents → first chapter. 登录 shows the source's `loginUi` form; web-page logins report that WebView is not supported yet.

发现 lists sources with explore categories (filter field and group chips); one source expands at a time into its categories, sized by Legado's flex hints, and a category opens a paged book list. Each source row's menu offers 在此书源搜索, 刷新分类, and 编辑书源. The header search opens 搜索: a keyword field, a scope chip (all enabled sources, groups, or one source), 精准搜索, keyword history, a progress bar with 停止, results folded by name and author with a source count, and a collapsed list of sources that failed. An online book's page shows the chosen source's details, 换源 among the sources that found it, whether the book is already on the shelf, a 目录 row that opens a full-screen contents page laid out like the local one, and 加入书架 or 移出书架 with 阅读. 阅读 and choosing a chapter open the reader; leaving the reader returns to that page over the search results or category list it came from.

## Reading controls

Phone novels default to one page. Desktop defaults to a centered single page and offers double-page and scroll reading. Screens account for the status bar, navigation bar, and display cutout; Android reports these insets natively because edge-to-edge WebViews do not reliably provide CSS safe-area values. Enter the reader with toolbars hidden; tapping the center toggles them, while dragging or selecting text does not. Dragging or wheel-scrolling the continuous view hides them so overlay bars never cover the passage being read, and its position indicator sits at the screen edge and appears only while scrolling; Escape also hides them before leaving the reader. The reading menu follows Legado 3.26.7 with GripRead styling. The top bar has back, the book title over the current chapter, then TXT目录规则, 设置编码, and More (add bookmark, update directory, page animation). The bottom has previous chapter, an in-chapter progress slider, and next chapter above the tools chosen in the 底部工具栏 dialog from More (by default Search, Automatic Page Turning, 朗读, Contents, 界面, and a day/night toggle). Contents and Search are full-screen pages: Contents has 目录 and 书签 tabs, collapsible volumes, the current chapter highlighted and centred, filtering, and TXT rule, long-chapter, reverse, and length options; Search lists matches with their chapter, emphasized context, and position, and an opened result shows a bar for the previous or next match. The rule and encoding choices are dialogs. 界面 is a bottom sheet following Legado's style dialog with immediate preview: font size, page animation, reading mode, colour presets with day and night variants (editable, with new presets), and sub-views for text (fonts with import, weight, letter, line, and paragraph spacing, indent), a small centred margin dialog over the live page, without blur and with the reading menu hidden (body, header, and footer margins and divider lines), header and footer slots, chapter titles, and more settings (keep screen on, hide system bars, volume-key paging, menu following the page colours, screen orientation, nine tap zones); a preset can also use day and night background images. On Android a brightness bar with a follow-system button sits at the left edge while the menu is open. Search has a regular-expression toggle; Automatic Page Turning starts at once with the 界面 page animation. Selecting text raises a floating bar with 复制, 书签 (a dialog with an optional note), 搜索, 替换 (the replace rule editor prefilled with the selection), and 分享. While automatic turning is on, a tap on the centre shows a non-blocking automatic panel (state, interval, pause or resume, menu, exit) instead of the reading menu. More in the top bar adds the per-book 替换净化 switch, the replace rule page (enable, edit with a test field, reorder, import and export Legado JSON), 简繁转换 (a dialog), 底部工具栏, and 书籍详情 (also opened by tapping the title); the bottom bar has 朗读, a sheet with paragraph skip, play/pause, stop, speed, pitch, voice, and a sleep timer, and a small pill keeps control while it reads. The reader has no separate app-theme setting. On the shelf, the group bar shows 全部, 在读, 未读, 读完, and user groups; a long press or right-click on a book opens its full-screen detail page (cover, metadata, progress, reading time, introduction, group choice, contents, editing with cover, and removal; leaving the contents it opened without choosing a chapter returns to the detail page), and 更多 offers adding files, importing a folder, refresh, layout, sort, group management, reading records, 云书架, and 回收站. The detail page has an action row: 编辑, the cloud action (上传到云书架, or 已在云书架 with 更新云端信息 and 从云书架删除), and 移出书架 with 移到回收站, 删除（保留云端文件）, and 本地与云端一起删除 behind a confirmation; a book in the 回收站 shows 移回书架. Shelf covers of uploaded books carry a small cloud mark. 云书架 lists the WebDAV book folder with upload-all, download, and delete; 备份与恢复 holds the WebDAV account (address, account, password, 子文件夹, 设备名称, and a latency-reporting connection test), 同步阅读进度 and 同步增强, the local backup folder, daily automatic backup, backup to WebDAV or a file, the 恢复忽略列表, and restore from WebDAV (listing each backup's device), a file, or a recovery point after a preview. The reader asks before jumping to another device's progress. Double-page mode falls back automatically in narrow viewports; chapter endings may leave unused space. Source switching and downloads remain under More when supported. Taps and swipes work anywhere on the reading screen, including margins, the header and footer, and the space beside a centred column. Taps follow Legado's nine zones (left column and top band go back, right column and bottom band go forward, the centre opens the menu), and with the menu open any tap closes it. Swipes turn pages wherever they start, including over text, and with curl, slide, or cover the page follows the finger; releasing after moving back cancels the turn. Holding a press still starts text selection. Volume keys, arrow keys, and Space also turn a page/spread in paginated modes; scroll uses native vertical movement and viewport-sized keyboard navigation. Suppress reading shortcuts inside controls and while selecting text. One panel state owns the reader tools, and closing a panel restores focus without turning a page. Returning to the shelf is immediate while accepted writes continue in the application; real save errors use an unobtrusive retry notice. Detailed navigation and automatic-reading behavior belongs to [Reader](../subsystems/reader.md).

A contents selection navigates to that chapter. Reader layout changes follow the [locator contract](../subsystems/reader.md). Loading and sync indicators remain outside the text; remote progress produces a user-selectable action instead of an automatic jump.

RSS articles scroll continuously and show title, source, date, author, and content, with a bar for the previous and next article, 全文, read, star, and 原文. On a phone, subscriptions (全部文章, folding groups, unread counts), a list's articles (全部, 未读, 星标, search, a long press for read and star), and the article open one over another. Preserve list position when returning from an article and retain selected columns on desktop. Content extraction and article-state behavior belong to [RSS](../subsystems/rss.md).

## States and accessibility

Design every screen together with its loading, empty, partial-failure, offline, and success states. Errors offer a relevant retry or recovery action while retaining usable content. Maintain visible keyboard focus, touch-accessible controls, understandable labels, and consistent back/dismiss behavior.

Each page specification includes the [motion contract](motion.md) alongside layout and states. UI components consume explicit data and event interfaces; they do not invent synchronization, storage, source, or AI behavior.

## Acceptance

Verify phone and desktop layouts, keyboard focus, touch controls, modal focus restoration, empty/error states, and preserved reading/list context. Evaluate motion and reduced-motion behavior using the cases owned by the motion specification.

Bars at the bottom of the screen cover the system gesture bar too: full-screen pages pad their frame (not their scroll body, whose padding would let text scroll under the bar) by the bottom inset, and the main tabs keep an opaque strip behind the gesture bar under the floating dock. 阅读记录 shows today's and total time, a GitHub-style activity matrix of the last 26 weeks (Monday-first columns, five levels by minutes read, today outlined, a tapped day's time below), the last seven days, and the books read; deleted books and online books read without adding them stay listed with 已删除 or 不在书架 and explain why they cannot be opened. Generated covers size their title from the cover's width so small covers show it whole.

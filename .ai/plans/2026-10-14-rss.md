# Stage 8: RSS reader

Status: in-progress

## Outcome

订阅 is an ordinary dedicated RSS reader ([RSS](../subsystems/rss.md)): feeds by address or discovery, OPML, article lists with unread and starred, reading with full text and offline images, and refreshing on entering, on request, and on an interval. Subscriptions, read, and starred articles sync through the server and go into backups; the server refreshes feeds on its own schedule and hands articles to connected devices; the web version reads RSS too.

The work happens on a local branch `rss`, cut from `dev` while 0.2.1 waits for the user's device test, and merges after 0.2.1 is released, to ship as 0.3.0.

## Scope

**Included:**
1. The core: storage (schema 23), fetching and parsing, sanitizing, discovery, OPML, full text, images, and commands.
2. The frontend: `FeedService`, `FeedStore`, and the 订阅 screens for phones and wide screens.
3. Sync records, backups, the server's refresh schedule and routes, and refreshing through the server.

**Excluded:** Legado `rssSource` rules, Android background refresh and notifications, podcasts and media enclosures.

## Interfaces

`FeedService` and the commands listed in [RSS](../subsystems/rss.md); the sync records in the [article-state decision](../notes/implemented/2026-10-14-rss-article-state.md); `/api/rss/refresh` and `/api/rss/articles` on the [server](../subsystems/server.md).

## Acceptance criteria

The acceptance list in [RSS](../subsystems/rss.md), and the phone and desktop layouts checked in a browser.

## Verification

- `pnpm check` passes, with core tests for parsing, refresh, discovery, OPML, sanitizing, full text, retention, paging, sync convergence, and backups, and a server test for refreshing through the server.
- Frontend tests cover the store and the phone flow.
- The preview build was checked at 390 px and 1280 px.
- Device testing is pending.

## Handoff

All three batches are implemented on `rss`. The user tests with a local APK when they have time; the branch merges into `dev` after 0.2.1 is released.

# 0.4.1: 订阅, second round

Status: in-progress

## Outcome

订阅 becomes the app's focus after the user's first test of `v0.4.1-beta.1`, which raised 18 points. They are done in four batches, each released as a beta of 0.4.1: interaction fixes and each feed's own settings; regular-expression filters, large article counts, and feed search with RSSHub detection; accounts on Google Reader API services (FreshRSS, Miniflux, Inoreader, and others), kept beside local feeds; and one place in 设置 for AI models and speech, whose summary and read-aloud features come in the next minor version. Behavior is owned by [RSS](../subsystems/rss.md).

## Scope

**Batch 1 (beta.2):**
1. A custom refresh interval (5–1440 minutes).
2. 订阅设置 without the tab toggle, which stays in 外观设置.
3. 管理订阅 with 添加订阅 and without a link to 订阅设置.
4. Back closing an article before its list.
5. A long-press menu at the finger: 以上/以下标为已读 and 保持未读.
6. 划出屏幕时标为已读.
7. Read articles dimmed.
8. 订阅详情: name, group, a new address, 启用更新, and the feed's own interval, retention, and 全文.
9. 分组管理.
10. 订阅文章 in 数据管理.
11. 全文 reset by the peer: browser headers, a second try, and the server reading the page.

**Batch 2 (beta.3):**
1. Filters, applied when articles are stored, on the device and on the server alike.
2. Large lists: windowed rendering, FTS5 search, a picture cache limit, and a test with 50,000 articles.
3. Feed search: the Feedly directory and RSSHub Radar rules, with articles previewed before subscribing.

**Batch 3 (beta.4):** Accounts on Google Reader API services: a section per account, changes written back and queued while offline, passwords only on the device.

**Batch 4 (beta.5):** AI 模型 (the translation engines moved out to be shared) and 语音 (the system voice settings made global; online engines configured and previewed only).

**Excluded:** Background refresh on Android; summaries and online read-aloud themselves (the next minor version).

## Interfaces

Schema 30 adds each feed's own settings and `id_base`, `rss_groups`, `keep_unread`, and `rss_cleared`. New commands: `list_feed_groups`, `save_feed_groups`, `rename_feed_group`, `remove_feed_group`, `mark_read_range`. `update_feed` takes a new address, enabled, and options and answers the feed. `refresh_feeds` takes `due`. The server adds `POST /api/rss/fulltext` and refreshes feeds by their own intervals. Sync adds the feed's settings to `feed:` records and `rss-group:` records; older records read as enabled with default settings.

## Acceptance criteria

The acceptance list in [RSS](../subsystems/rss.md); each batch's screens checked at 390 px and 1280 px.

## Verification

- Batch 1: `pnpm check` passed. Core tests cover:
  - a feed moved to a new address across two devices and a server;
  - per-feed intervals and stopped feeds;
  - group order, renames, merges, and removal;
  - ranges and 保持未读;
  - clearing articles;
  - 全文 falling back to the server.

  A server test covers the server refusing a local page. Interface tests cover back, the long-press menu, 管理订阅 with 订阅详情 and 分组管理, the custom interval, and marking read on scrolling. Android clippy passed.
- Batches 2–4: not started.

## Handoff

Batch 1 is on `dev`. The reported connection reset was not reproduced on the host. Browser headers and a retry are in place, and while connected the server reads the page instead; the user's device test decides whether that is enough.

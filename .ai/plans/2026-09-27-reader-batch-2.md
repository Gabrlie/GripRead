# TXT reader Batch 2: chapters and reading modes

Status: in-progress

## Outcome

Deliver the next batch of the [TXT reader task](2026-09-24-txt-reader-modes-and-settings.md): chapter navigation and progress, single-page and double-page reading, bounded continuous scrolling, and mode-appropriate effects and automatic reading. Repair the three Batch 1 review findings before extending their consumers. Acceptance targets the macOS app at desktop and narrow-window sizes.

Behavior belongs to [Reader](../subsystems/reader.md), [interaction design](../design/interaction.md), and [motion design](../design/motion.md). The [navigation and modes proposal](../notes/proposed/2026-09-27-reader-navigation-and-modes.md) records the shared contract to establish before implementation. Update those behavior owners as each slice ships; this plan does not claim that the proposed modes already work.

## Scope

Allowed areas are `src/reader`, `src/components/Reader`, preference/runtime integration in `src/application`, `src/services`, generated bindings, reader styles, Rust models/commands/storage, associated tests and native fixtures, and their owning documentation. Font/image assets, schema 9, complete typography, information bars, chapter page counts, battery state, and toolbar customization remain in later batches. EPUB, online sources, RSS, sync, AI, and Android/Windows acceptance remain outside this task.

### Delivery order

1. **Repair Batch 1.** Clip an overlapping search highlight to the visible page instead of dropping a negative local start. Make an active settings save respect a newer debounce timer without delaying explicit flush. Retain Contents mode for the current book when its panel closes. Cover cross-page highlights and slow-save/edit/flush races with regression tests; verify the panel choice through its consumer. Commit these repairs separately from new features.
2. **Establish shared contracts.** Add chapter-range derivation and exact UTF-16 offset resolution; extend preferences additively and regenerate bindings. Define a mode-aware controller snapshot, reading anchor, bounded content ownership, and presentation completion/cancellation. Migrate existing single-page consumers together, preserving current behavior before enabling other modes. Record semantics and links to authoritative source definitions in the decision note; do not copy type declarations into documentation.
3. **Ship chapter controls in single-page mode.** Derive current/adjacent chapters from the accepted TOC and active content version. Replace the bottom row with previous chapter, chapter progress, and next chapter; move whole-book progress into Contents. Handle preface, empty/no directory, final chapter, rule changes, and chapter-end seeking. Keep ordinary page turns on side taps, swipes, and keyboard actions. Update native helpers that currently treat `reader-next`, `reader-previous`, and the bottom slider as page/whole-book controls; assertions must keep exercising the intended behavior.
4. **Ship double-page mode.** Measure each column at its actual width and publish one spread per navigation operation, including across content-window boundaries. Add the mode selector, responsive single-page fallback, and restoration of the selected mode when the viewport widens. Establish instant spread navigation first, including lone final pages and previous-spread reconstruction after restart.
5. **Ship continuous scrolling.** Add bounded block loading in both directions, anchor-preserving prepend/eviction, visible-text locator tracking, and restoration through the same controller and save worker. Connect chapter seeks, search highlights, bookmarks, reflow, encoding replacement, loading failure/retry, and mode switching. Verify manual scroll and exact passage restoration before adding automatic scroll.
6. **Complete effects and automatic reading.** Support instant/curl/slide/fade in paginated modes, with spread-aware presentation and reduced-motion behavior. Retain page interval settings for one page/spread at a time; add independent scroll speed for continuous mode. Share pause/exit ownership across both automatic paths. Run the combined regression, native, visual, and bounded-render acceptance below before declaring Batch 2 complete.

Each numbered slice is a reviewable commit boundary; a large slice may use multiple cohesive commits. The shared-contract slice precedes parallel implementation. After it lands, chapter controls, paginated presentation, and scroll layout can be developed against those contracts, but controller/types, `ReaderView`, shared styles, and native support helpers each need one integration owner. Do not modify a shared interface independently in multiple work streams.

## Acceptance criteria

- All three review findings are resolved. Cross-page search highlights remain visible; slow writes do not bypass the inactivity delay; panel close, leaving, and normal exit flush the latest settings; Contents retains its selection within the book session.
- Chapter ranges cover the body without overlaps or gaps, including preface, no headings, one heading, duplicate starts, and an empty book. A chapter seek at 0% reaches its start; 100% shows its end without changing the current chapter. Emoji at range/page/window boundaries never split. Previous/next chapter disable at the outer boundaries, and a slow or failed TOC build does not block readable text.
- A single page or spread never displays another chapter as a side effect of a 100% seek. Normal forward/backward reading still crosses chapters continuously. A spread is committed and saved once, never as two observable page turns. Odd chapter endings have no duplicated or invented right-page text.
- Switching between single-page, double-page, and scroll preserves the same passage. Test 899, 900, and 901 CSS px viewports, a narrow viewport, late font loading, and resizing during navigation. Responsive fallback does not overwrite the stored mode. Toolbar/panel visibility causes no reflow or locator change.
- Scroll prepend and recycling keep the anchored text at the same viewport position. Long paragraphs, blank lines, Chinese/English, and emoji cross block boundaries without missing/repeated text or extra paragraph spacing. Search and bookmarks restore the target passage; manual scroll is saved on pause/leave/exit and restored after process restart. A final chapter shorter than the viewport stays current at 0%, 100%, and after restart; trailing alignment space has no locator or progress contribution.
- Failed or delayed adjacent reads retain usable content and offer retry. Repeated seek/mode/reflow requests reject stale results. Applying a rule while a chapter-end seek is delayed invalidates the old directory generation before that seek can publish, retaining the current passage. Encoding changes cannot publish old text or chapter ranges. Selection remains usable while automatic reading is paused and blocks under selection are protected from recycling.
- Effects finish or cancel under rapid navigation, resize, mode changes, panel opening, and reduced motion. Automatic reading waits for page/spread completion, stops at the book end, pauses under all existing interruption conditions, and never resumes or catches up elapsed background time automatically. Exit drains its final anchor before completing persistence.
- For 1, 10, and 50 MiB fixtures, body windows, rendered blocks, and animation clones remain within the explicit controller budget regardless of book length. Verify bounds after sustained forward and backward reading, not just first render. Record first readable render, mode-switch/reflow time, and peak rendered content; no whole-book layout or re-import is permitted.

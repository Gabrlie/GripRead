# M0 closeout and M1 TXT reading

Status: in-progress

## Outcome

Keep the 手不释卷 reader shell while completing the remaining M0 storage, reader, and macOS evidence work, then deliver the first M1 TXT capabilities: encoding detection and switching, conservative table of contents, bookmarks, and cancellable literal search. The release boundary remains defined by [the product requirements](../product/requirements.md) and [the roadmap](roadmap.md).

## Scope

Included work covers Rust TXT decoding and storage migration, staged re-decode publication and recovery, generated IPC contracts, application-owned decode and search cancellation, reader integration, TOC/bookmark/search panels, PageFlip cleanup, and native test isolation. The work preserves LF-normalized UTF-16 locators, Book and BookAsset identity, the existing visual shell, and explicit error states. EPUB, Legado sources, RSS, WebDAV, AIGC, full shelf management, and Android/Windows acceptance remain excluded.

## Interfaces

Rust models remain the sole IPC source and generate `src/types/generated.ts`. Library commands own TXT import, source re-association, and re-decode tasks; reader commands own content windows, TOC, bookmarks, and search. Re-decode retains raw source bytes, stages normalized UTF-8 and sparse indexes outside the database mutex, and publishes through `txt_decode_journal`. Search requests carry an optional operation identifier so the reader can cancel stale scans. Schema version 5 adds decode recovery state without changing existing Book or BookAsset IDs.

## Acceptance criteria

- UTF-8, BOM, GB18030/GBK, and UTF-16 TXT inputs decode to normalized LF text; manual selection persists and failed/cancelled re-decodes keep the prior version.
- UTF-16 locator windows, TOC entries, bookmarks, and search matches remain valid around emoji and window boundaries; stale search work cannot update a newer query.
- Repeated normalized imports do not replace an existing book's raw source or selected encoding; interrupted publication recovers on the next open.
- M0 native core, restart, failure, instant, animated, and crash-recovery flows remain deterministic; system-picker and native window close/quit evidence stay explicitly tracked until recorded.

## Verification

The base implementation passed `pnpm check`, including 26 Rust tests and 44 frontend tests, and the ordinary macOS build. The repair pass adds stale-locator, in-process re-decode recovery, manual-decode re-import, Unicode-safe bookmark, and search paging/cancellation coverage. Current verification has 27 Rust tests and 45 frontend tests passing, the ordinary macOS debug build passing, and the full native suite passing: core 4, failures 5, restart 1, instant/animated interaction 4 each, M1 TXT 3, and crash marker/recovery. Native phases use isolated fixture IPC imports; system-picker selection/cancellation and native window close/quit remain unverified.

## Handoff

Review the combined storage and IPC diff first, then inspect the native evidence and remaining source re-association behavior. The reader now keeps a stale locator until an explicit navigation action, and re-decode publication failures restore the previous asset before returning. The durable raw-source and decode-journal decision is recorded in [the TXT M1 note](../notes/implemented/2026-09-18-txt-m1.md). Do not mark M0 or the wider reader specifications complete until picker evidence and their remaining acceptance cases are complete.
